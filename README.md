# 📊 Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard – Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer churn is an important business challenge because losing existing customers can directly affect revenue and long-term growth.

This project presents an interactive **Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI**. The dashboard analyzes customer behavior, churn patterns, retention, contract types, tenure, customer risk, and monthly charges.

The main purpose of this project is to identify the factors associated with customer churn and provide meaningful business insights that can help organizations improve customer retention and reduce churn.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The major objectives of this project are:

- Analyze the overall customer churn rate.
- Identify the number of churned and retained customers.
- Understand churn behavior across different contract types.
- Analyze customer churn according to tenure.
- Identify high-risk customers.
- Compare monthly charges between retained and churned customers.
- Analyze the impact of online services on customer behavior.
- Generate actionable business recommendations from the analysis.

## 📊 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Customer Retention Overview

The customer retention section provides an overview of the total customer base and helps compare retained and churned customers.

The dashboard shows that a significant portion of customers remain with the company, while a considerable percentage of customers have also churned.

---

### 2. Churn Rate by Tenure Band

Customers were analyzed across different tenure groups:

- **0–6 Months**
- **6–12 Months**
- **12+ Months**

This analysis helps identify how customer churn changes as customers spend more time with the company.

Understanding tenure-based churn can help businesses focus retention strategies on customers who are more likely to leave.

---

### 3. Churned Customers by Contract Type

The dashboard compares churned customers across different contract types:

| Contract Type | Churned Customers |
|---|---:|
| Month-to-Month | 1,655 |
| One Year | 166 |
| Two Year | 48 |

The analysis clearly shows that **Month-to-Month customers have the highest number of churned customers**.

This indicates that customers with flexible contracts may be more likely to leave compared with customers committed to longer-term contracts.

---

### 4. Customer Risk Analysis

The dashboard categorizes customers based on their risk level.

The analysis shows:

- **Normal Risk:** 4,541 customers
- **High Risk:** 633 customers

High-risk customers represent an important segment for retention campaigns because proactive engagement may help prevent future churn.

---

### 5. Monthly Charges Analysis

The dashboard compares average monthly charges for retained and churned customers.

The visualization indicates that:

- Retained customers have an average monthly charge of approximately **61**
- Churned customers have an average monthly charge of approximately **74**

This suggests that customers with higher monthly charges may require additional attention because higher pricing or service costs could potentially influence churn.

---

### 6. Online Security & Online Backup Analysis

The dashboard evaluates monthly charges across customer service categories such as:

- Online Security
- Online Backup
- No Internet Service

This analysis helps understand the relationship between additional services and customer spending.

---

### 7. Customer Type Analysis

Customer groups were also compared based on customer type.

The dashboard helps identify differences in monthly charges between:

- Existing Customers
- New Customers

This can help businesses understand the value and behavior of different customer segments.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

Based on the dashboard analysis, several important insights were identified:

### 🔹 High Churn Rate

The overall churn rate is **26.54%**, which indicates that customer retention is an important business concern.

### 🔹 Month-to-Month Customers Are More Vulnerable

Month-to-Month customers account for the majority of churned customers, with **1,655 churned customers**.

### 🔹 High-Risk Customers Need Attention

There are **633 high-risk customers**, making this an important segment for targeted retention strategies.

### 🔹 Higher Monthly Charges May Be Associated With Churn

Churned customers show higher average monthly charges than retained customers, suggesting that pricing and service value should be investigated.

### 🔹 Tenure Matters

Analyzing customers according to tenure helps businesses understand when customers are more likely to churn and when retention efforts should be initiated.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following strategies can help reduce customer churn:

### 1. Focus on Month-to-Month Customers

Businesses should create targeted offers and incentives to encourage month-to-month customers to move toward longer-term contracts.

### 2. Target High-Risk Customers

High-risk customers can be identified and contacted through personalized offers, discounts, service improvements, and proactive customer support.

### 3. Improve Customer Onboarding

New customers should receive strong onboarding support during their initial months to build trust and improve long-term retention.

### 4. Review Pricing and Monthly Charges

Since churned customers have higher average monthly charges, businesses should review pricing plans and ensure that customers receive sufficient value for the services they pay for.

### 5. Encourage Additional Services

Relevant services such as online security and backup can be promoted to customers where they provide genuine value and improve customer engagement.
